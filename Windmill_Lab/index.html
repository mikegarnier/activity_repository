Model Windmill Investigation

Build a model windmill, turn on the box fan, and test how blade size, shape, material, number of blades, and blade angle affect energy output.

Windmill Design Controls

4 blades
45° pitch to wind
Fan power
Suggested student questions: Which blade count produces the most energy? Is a bigger blade always better? Does wood perform differently than cardboard? Is there a best blade angle?
This simulation is intentionally simplified for classroom testing. It behaves logically, but it is not a full engineering model.
